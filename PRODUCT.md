# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friends, partners, and small listening groups who want to hear the same lossless playback at the same time — often late, often in different cities. The host is already playing music in another app (Music, a browser, a DAW). Listeners should join in under a minute, no account, no port forwarding.

## Product Purpose

Sonora is a cross-platform desktop listening room. A host picks a window or app, streams that audio losslessly over peer-to-peer QUIC, and shares a short room code. Listeners join from any network. Relays kick in when NAT blocks a direct path. Success is: the room feels like sitting in the same room, not like a call.

## Brand Personality

Late-night hi-fi. Quiet, precise, unhurried. Three words: **hushed, filament, sure**. The interface should get out of the way of the music. Copy is short and concrete — room codes, sources, listeners — never “unlock your audio journey.”

## Anti-references

- Discord Stage / voice-channel chrome and neon presence lists
- Spotify Connect popovers and playlist marketing
- DJ / DAW skins: skeuomorphic knobs, rainbow EQ walls, beat grids
- Glassmorphic SaaS dashboards and hero-metric tool layouts
- Gaming overlay aesthetic (RGB, kill-feed typography)

## Design Principles

- The music is the product; chrome is a dimmed instrument panel.
- One job per screen: choose host or join, pick a source, or listen.
- Make the room code unmistakable — it is the only invitation.
- Tell the truth about the path: live, connecting, direct, relay, error.
- Never decorate with fake audio theater (idle graphic EQs, vinyl dust).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA for text and controls. Visible focus. Room state is never color-only (label + meter). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Volume and source lists are fully keyboard operable. Do not flash the meter.
